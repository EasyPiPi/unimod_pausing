# Direct evidence and quotes on paused RNAPII and +1 nucleosome

All these papers are consistent with a positive spatial/functional relationship between promoter-proximal paused RNAPII and a positioned +1 nucleosome. Major points of evidence include:

1. The +1 nucleosome forms a physical elongation barrier. Pol II pauses when encountering it based on Cryo-EM and in vitro transcription assays;
2. In vivo perturbations of NELF show that Pol II pausing shifts to a second pause region located closer to or within the +1 nucleosomal dyad;
3. Genome-wide evidence shows good correlation between well-positioned +1 nucleosomes and stronger pausing.

Here are relevant quotes from key papers:

- **Jimeno-González et al., Nucleic Acids Res. 2015**  

  > “When a +1 nucleosome is strongly positioned, elongating RNAPII has a tendency to stall at the promoter-proximal region, recruits more negative elongation factor (NELF) and produces less mRNA.”

- **Santana et al., Nucleic Acids Res. 2024**  
  Evidence: NELF perturbation changes both the pausing signature and +1 nucleosome architecture in the same direction, consistent with functional co-alignment.

  > “Our study confirmed that the first nucleosome encountered during initiation and early elongation is highly positioned with respect to the major TSS.”

- **DeBerardine et al., Nature Communications 2023**

  > "This matches a previous finding that acute NELF depletion causes a downstream shift in promoter-proximal Pol II density into a defined “second pause” region in which the active site of Pol II appears roughly coincident with the +1 nucleosome dyad. While more subtle in our experimental system, we find that our “second pause” also coincides with the average dyad position of the +1 nucleosome (Fig. 2f inset), which in Drosophila averages ~140 bp from the TSS."

- **Aoi et al., Mol Cell 2020**  
  > “Here, we show that upon rapid depletion of NELF, RNA Pol II fails to be released into gene bodies, stopping instead around the +1 nucleosomal dyad-associated region.”

- **Luse et al., Nucleic Acids Res. 2020**  
  > “A … assay … demonstrated that a +1 nucleosome is present downstream of paused Pol II.”

  > “Model summarizing our findings. Promoter recognition occurs through interaction of TFIID with sequences surrounding the TSS and with a positioned +1 nucleosome. Pausing is influenced by both downstream sequence and through blockage by the downstream +1 nucleosome.”

- **Naganuma et al., Science Advances 2025** 
  
  Evidence: Cryo-EM of paused RNAPII at promoter-proximal sites showing direct contacts/arrangements with the +1 nucleosome, which provides a direct physical model explaining why paused RNAPII should localize immediately upstream / in contact with the +1 nucleosome.

  > “The metazoan transcription elongation complex (EC) of RNA polymerase II (RNAPII) generally stalls between the transcription start site and the first (+1) nucleosome.”

- **Akatsu et al., J. Biol. Chem. 2023**  

  > “In the present study, we performed cryo-electron microscopy analyses of RNAPII–nucleosome complexes paused at a major nucleosomal pausing site, SHL(−1).”

- **Abril-Garrido et al., Molecular Cell 2023**  
  > “Here, we establish a model system to show that a promoter-proximal +1 nucleosome can reduce RNA synthesis in vivo and in vitro, and we analyze its structural basis.”

- **Mavrich et al., Cell 2008 (Drosophila nucleosome organization)**  
  > “At thousands of genes, RNA polymerase II engages the +1 nucleosome and pauses.”

- **Schones et al., Cell 2008 (Dynamic Regulation of Nucleosome Positioning in the Human Genome)**  
  > “We find that nucleosome phasing relative to the transcription start sites is directly correlated to RNA polymerase II (Pol II) binding.”
